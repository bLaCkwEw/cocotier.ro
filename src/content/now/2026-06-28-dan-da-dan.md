I immediately good hooked on Dan Da Dan. Momo & Ken have great synergy. The story so far is genuinely absurd, but that's what makes it sooo goooddd!!

A detail that I really respected and enjoyed was the fact that they never made a gag of the few moments when the protagonists happened to end up embracing each other.

They would just share a small intimate moment, then go back to living in their absurd little world. It felt like coming up for air after being overwhelmed by the world around you.
